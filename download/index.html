HOW TO INSTALL

Get started in three steps

These apps are provided directly by MAo. No MAo Studio account is required.

01

Download

Choose the Windows (.exe) or Android (.apk) button and save the official installer on your device.

02

Review & install

Check the installer source, then follow Windows Setup or your Android installation prompts.

03

Open MAo

Launch MAo from your desktop or phone to explore our public website and updates.

Installation and security

On Windows, this installer is not digitally code-signed, so SmartScreen may show an Unknown publisher notice. On Android, MAo is not yet on Google Play and your device may request installation approval. Only proceed after verifying the file came from the official MAo website or GitHub release. Do not disable device security or Play Protect.

Both apps need an internet connection for the latest content. The Android release uses a temporary self-signed signing certificate; a future upgrade may require uninstalling the older APK until we configure permanent signing. Neither app provides CMS access.
