About us

Women and young farmers at the heart of change

MAo is a nongovernmental organization founded to support women and young farmers in Tanzania. Its constitution sets out an ambition to address food insecurity, climate change, losses from limited farming knowledge, lack of market access, and youth unemployment.

The constitution places the head office in Uyole, Mbeya District, Mbeya Region. It describes Mainland Tanzania and Zanzibar as the intended area of operation.

These details come from the first edition of the MAo constitution, dated 2024.
Our focus

Pathways to productive farming

These are goals stated in the constitution. We will distinguish them from completed projects when verified activity reports are available.

01

Farmer training

Equip women and young people with skills for productive, modern farming.

02

Sustainable agriculture

Promote farming practices that respond to climate change.

03

Crop value addition

Build knowledge in crop care, processing, and value addition.

04

Market access

Help members reach domestic and international markets for their produce.

05

Entrepreneurship

Offer education that can strengthen household income and livelihoods.

06

Partnerships

Work with government, international organizations, and development partners on agricultural initiatives.

Our vision

“Kilimo ni Uhai”

MAo believes productive, sustainable, modern agriculture is a foundation for community and economic development in Tanzania.

Our mission

Knowledge and opportunity

Provide training, resources, and employment opportunities for women and young people to build economic independence through valuable, sustainable agriculture.
